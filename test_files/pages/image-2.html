Calandrinia grandiflora (Rock purslane)
Calandrinia grandiflora, commonly known as rock purslane, is a species of flowering plant in the Portulacaceae family. It is native to the western United States and is commonly found in rocky areas, such as cliffs and rocky hillsides.
