White Stork in the blue sky of Ukraine
The white stork, seen here soaring in the peaceful blue skies of Ukraine, is a large migratory wading bird known for its long legs, long red bill, and broad wings. It often soars on thermal currents with its neck outstretched and is widely seen across Europe and parts of Asia and Africa. This bird is closely associated with open wetlands, grasslands, and farmland, where it forages for insects, frogs, and small reptiles.
