Tropaeolum, commonly known as nasturtium
Tropaeolum, commonly known as nasturtium, is a genus of flowering plants native to parts of Central and South America. These plants are widely grown as ornamentals and are especially valued for their vivid blossoms, rounded leaves, and easy-growing nature in gardens and containers. Many species and cultivated varieties belong to the family Tropaeolaceae, a small plant family best known for nasturtiums themselves.
Nasturtiums are also notable for being edible: their leaves, flowers, and immature seeds have a peppery flavor that has made them a familiar ingredient in salads and garnishes. Popular in horticulture since at least the early modern period, they attract pollinators such as bees and hummingbirds in suitable regions. With their blend of ornamental appeal and culinary usefulness, nasturtiums remain a favorite in both decorative and practical planting.
