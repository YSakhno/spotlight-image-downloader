American Robin in Golden Gate Park
The American Robin is one of the most familiar songbirds across North America, often spotted hopping across lawns in search of food. It is recognized by its warm orange-red breast, grayish-brown back, and distinctive white markings around the eyes. Despite its name, the American robin is not closely related to the European Robin; the name was inspired by a superficial resemblance noticed by early settlers.
Widespread and adaptable, American robins inhabit forests, gardens, parks, and suburban areas, thriving in close proximity to human activity. Their diet shifts with the seasons—earthworms and insects are staples in spring and summer, while fruits and berries become more important in fall and winter. Known for their clear, melodious song, robins are often among the first birds to sing at dawn and are widely regarded as a harbinger of spring.
