Zonal geranium (or garden geranium)
The vivid blooms of Pelargonium × hortorum, commonly known as garden geraniums, stand out in this close-up composition. Widely cultivated as ornamental plants, zonal geraniums are prized for their dense flower heads and long blooming season. Native to Southern Africa through their parent species, these plants thrive in sunny conditions and are a common feature in gardens and window boxes, where their bold colors and resilience make them especially popular.
